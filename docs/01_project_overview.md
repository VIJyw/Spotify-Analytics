# Spotify Global Streaming Analysis 2024
**Product Analytics Portfolio Project**

## 🎯 Executive Summary

This analysis challenges conventional assumptions about music streaming success by revealing that **popularity doesn't equal engagement**. Using 2024 global streaming data, I uncovered that mainstream genres like Pop show surprisingly poor retention metrics (23.13% skip rates), while less popular genres like Reggaeton demonstrate exceptional user engagement (309.90 average replay rates).

---

## 📊 Business Context

Streaming platforms optimize for total streams, but this metric alone doesn't capture user satisfaction or long-term retention. Understanding which content genuinely engages users versus what simply generates volume is critical for:

- **Algorithm optimization** and recommendation systems
- **Content acquisition strategy** and artist partnerships
- **User retention** and subscription value maximization
- **Regional content strategy** and localization efforts

---

##  Key Business Questions ?

1. **Do popular genres actually drive engagement?**  
   Surface-level stream counts may mask poor completion and replay metrics

2. **How do cultural factors influence listening behavior?**  
   Geographic and linguistic patterns in skip rates suggest regional content strategy opportunities

3. **What defines high-value content?**  
   Identifying attributes of content that users actively choose to replay vs. skip

4. **Where should algorithms optimize?**  
   Balancing discovery with engagement quality

---

## 🔍 Analytical Approach

- **Engagement Metrics Development**: Created Replay Rate (avg plays per track) and Completion Rate calculations to measure true user satisfaction beyond stream volume

- **Efficiency Score**: Developed composite metric combining skip rate and replay performance to identify genuinely valuable content

- **Geographic Analysis**: Tested hypothesis around cultural/linguistic factors in skip behavior across European markets

- **Genre Performance Quadrants**: Mapped genres across popularity vs. engagement dimensions to identify strategic opportunities

---

## 💡 Key Findings

### 1. The Popularity Paradox
Pop music, despite being the most streamed genre, shows the worst engagement metrics:
- ⚠️ **23.13% skip rate** (above average)
- 📉 Low replay rates indicating minimal user loyalty
- 🎵 High volume driven by algorithmic promotion, not organic preference

### 2. Hidden Champions
Reggaeton and regional genres show exceptional engagement:
- ⭐ **309.90 average replay rate** (highest among major genres)
- ✅ Lower skip rates despite smaller overall audiences
- 💪 Strong indicators of passionate, loyal listener bases

### 3. Cultural Engagement Patterns
EDM and Hip Hop show **15-20% higher skip rates** in non-English speaking European countries (Germany, France, Italy) compared to English-speaking markets, suggesting:
- 🌍 Language barriers impact engagement with lyric-heavy genres
- 🎯 Opportunity for localized content strategy
- 🤖 Algorithm should weight cultural fit alongside popularity

### 4. The Engagement-Efficiency Gap
Many high-stream-count genres operate inefficiently:
- 💸 High promotion costs (playlist placement, recommendations)
- 📊 Low retention and replay value
- 🔄 Suggest need for algorithm rebalancing toward quality engagement

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

### For Product/Algorithm Teams:
- Reweight recommendation algorithms to balance discovery with completion likelihood
- Develop engagement-quality scores alongside stream volume metrics
- Implement cultural-linguistic matching in recommendation logic

### For Content Strategy:
- Invest in niche genres with high replay rates (better ROI per stream)
- Develop regional playlists optimized for cultural-linguistic fit
- Reconsider over-promotion of high-skip mainstream content

### For Growth/Retention:
- Surface high-engagement content earlier in user journey
- Test replay-rate-optimized playlists vs. popularity-based playlists
- Use completion rate as leading indicator for subscription renewal
